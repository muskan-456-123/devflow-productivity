# DreamDesk Room Progression

DreamDesk rewards completed work by making the room itself more personal, comfortable, and capable. The upgrades are intentionally visual rather than transactional: task completion changes the place where the user works.

| Completed tasks | Room chapter | Visible upgrade | Mood of the reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | First Light | The baseline tidy room is ready for a first meaningful action. | Quiet potential. |
| 1 | Screen Bloom | The laptop receives a warmer live screen, an illuminated keyboard, and a small desk marker. | The room is awake. |
| 2 | Green Corner | The plant gains new leaves and a small book stack appears beside the desk. | Work is taking root. |
| 3 | Better Seat | The chair gains a soft lavender cushion and headrest detail. | The room is supporting you. |
| 4 | Collected Desk | A pencil cup, framed desk card, and subtle warm desk light are added. | The space feels yours. |
| 5 | Skyline Finish | Window string lights and small floating sky motes appear; the room reaches its fully cared-for state. | A visible streak of momentum. |

The HUD displays the current chapter, the next room upgrade, and a brief earned-upgrade toast when a task is completed. Reopening a completed task lowers the room to the appropriate earlier chapter, making progress truthful and reversible.
